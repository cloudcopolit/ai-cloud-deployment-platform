# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the CloudPilot community a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful.
- Assume good intent while focusing discussion on ideas and evidence.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help keep collaboration inclusive.
- Use welcoming language and respect differing viewpoints.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, and other community interactions.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. Maintainers will review reports, protect the reporter's confidentiality where possible, and take any action they consider appropriate, including removing content or restricting participation.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards fairly and consistently.
