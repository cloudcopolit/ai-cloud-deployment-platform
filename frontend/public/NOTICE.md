## Trademarks

Brand logos in `brands/` belong to their owners (AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, GitHub, Python, PostgreSQL, and others). They are included to show which technologies CloudPilot works with. Follow each owner's brand guidelines, and do not use them in a way that implies endorsement.

The icons in `aws/` are generic drawings named after AWS services. They are not the official AWS Architecture Icons. To use the official set, download it from https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/icons/ and add it under `icons/aws-official/`.
